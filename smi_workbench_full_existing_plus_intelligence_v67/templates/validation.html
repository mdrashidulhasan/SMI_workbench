What this validation does — and does not do

Automatically scored

SMI title-search recall, identifier rescue, source identity, source coverage by method, and Structured Evidence controlled-vocabulary agreement (precision, recall, F1, exact agreement, normalized aliases, missing/extra terms, and vocabulary gaps).

Not treated as LaserAI gold-standard fields

Study design, statistical methods, sample size, comparator, effect estimate, and evidence sentence are richer SMI fields but are not present as gold-standard fields in this LaserAI export. They should be evaluated separately against source text or a curated gold set.
